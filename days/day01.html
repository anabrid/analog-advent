Welcome to the North Pole, where the countdown to Christmas has begun, and the air buzzes with the hum of bustling elves and the almost magical ticking machines. Meet our protagonist: A clever, overworked yet determined elf named Helmi. Helmi isn't just any elf; he's the one who keeps the whole operation from unraveling while Santa leans back in his rocking chair. Helmi is surrounded by a sea of problems: broken gift sorters, tangled communication systems, and a looming energy crisis from the overworked workshop grid. As the days tick by, Helmi must not only untangle the chaos but also uncover ingenious solutions-involving the latest trends in computing. Together, you'll join Helmi's journey, solving one problem at a time, and maybe even uncovering a few secrets about the magic that keeps Christmas alive.
